11. **Quiet fans** (Edson, 2026-10-21 17:20: "one of the most annoying things about this machine; I would work half a day on that"). The two base fans run at full speed from the moment the key turns, with no control from the board. Half-day mod: temperature-controlled fan driver (a PWM controller with a probe on the source's heatsink, or Noctua-class replacements of the same size with a thermostat), keeping full airflow during and after marking and near silence at idle. Raio can also help: it knows when a job runs, so a small USB relay/PWM board could switch between "job" and "idle" speeds, with a cool-down timer after the last pass. The noise also bleeds into recordings made nearby, so a quiet idle has a second payoff.
